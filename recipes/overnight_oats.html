Overnight Oats

Overnight oats are a simple and healthy breakfast option that can be prepared the night before. They are easy to make and can be customized with various toppings and flavors.

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup rolled oats
  • 1 cup milk (dairy or plant-based)
  • 1 tbsp chia seeds
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 tsp maple syrup or honey
  • Pinch of salt

Steps:

  1. In a jar or container, combine all ingredients and stir well.
  2. Cover and refrigerate overnight (or for at least 4 hours).
  3. In the morning, give it a good stir and add your favorite toppings.
